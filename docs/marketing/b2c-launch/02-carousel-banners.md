# Carousel Banners - Slide-by-Slide + Design Specs

**Where to build:** Canva, Figma, or Higgsfield. Each carousel below is ready to drop into a 1080x1350 (4:5, Instagram) frame. The design spec is the same MOCA mocha-latte world as the live page, so the carousel and the site feel like one brand.

## Shared design system (use on every slide)
- **Canvas:** 1080 x 1350 px (4:5). Safe margins 80px.
- **Palette:**
  - Background cream `#f9f4ee`
  - Card / surface `#ffffff`
  - Espresso (brand, headlines) `#5c3317`
  - Hot orange (accent, numbers, CTAs) `#c9622f`
  - Ink (body) `#3b1f0d`
  - Sub-text `#8a6a4a`
  - Good/green (savings, "cheapest") `#4a7c3f`
  - Bad/red (roaming, overpaying) `#b4472d`
- **Type:** Headlines = a bold serif (Frank Ruhl Libre for HE / a heavy serif for EN). Body = Assistant (HE) / Inter or system sans (EN). Big, few words.
- **Brand mark:** small MOCA bolt ⚡ top-left or bottom; brand-tag "השוואת eSIM / eSIM Compare".
- **Rounded everything:** 20-30px corners, soft shadow `0 6px 24px rgba(70,45,20,.10)`. Pill buttons.
- **Last slide is always the CTA** with the URL + the free/no-signup pill.
- **Swipe cue** on slide 1 (a small "→" or "1/7") to drive completion (completion = reach).

## Quick build tips
- **Make slide 1 do 80% of the work.** If the cover doesn't stop the scroll, the other slides never get seen.
- **One idea per slide, 5-9 words max.** Carousels die from text walls.
- **Numbers in orange, savings in green, pain in red.** Color-code the emotion.
- **Reuse the live UI.** Screenshot real result rows from `esim.mocaintel.com` and place them on white cards - authenticity beats illustration.
- **Export at 2x.** Post as 4:5 for max IG feed real estate. Repurpose the cover as the Reel thumbnail.
