# The Gary Vee Playbook - How To Actually Maximize Exposure At Launch

*Written in Gary's voice and philosophy, applied to MOCA eSIM Compare. Read it like he's sitting across from you.*

---

## First, the truth nobody wants to hear

You built a great product. Cool. Nobody cares yet. The product is not the bottleneck, **attention is the bottleneck.** You're not in the eSIM business, you're in the *attention* business, and the eSIM thing is how you make money once you have the attention.

Here's the good news: you're launching at the single best time in history to get attention for free. Organic reach on Reels, TikTok, and Shorts is the cheapest media buy that has ever existed. A no-name account can do a million views tomorrow if the content is good. So stop thinking "how do I get a budget" and start thinking "how do I make 50 pieces of content this week."

---

## The mindset shifts (do these or nothing else matters)

**1. Volume beats perfection. Always.**
You don't know what's going to hit. I don't know what's going to hit. The algorithm is the only judge. So you put out volume and you LISTEN. One in twenty videos pops. You can't get the one without the twenty. Stop polishing one video for a week. Put out one a day, minimum. Bank 20 in a weekend.

**2. Document, don't create.**
You don't need a studio. You ARE the content. Film yourself checking a price before a trip. Film the bill that made you build this. Film your screen. The most authentic founder doing the most boring real thing beats a glossy ad every time. People smell ads and scroll. They lean in for truth.

**3. Jab, jab, jab, right hook.**
Give, give, give, then ask. Your jabs = "here's a travel hack," "here's the price gap nobody told you about," "here's how eSIM works." Your right hook = "go to the site." If every post is a right hook, you're a billboard and people scroll. Be the most useful travel account in your niche for free, and the buying takes care of itself.

**4. Day-trade attention.**
Attention moves. Right now it's short-form video. Be where it's underpriced, be early to formats, and don't fall in love with one platform. The skill is noticing where attention is cheap THIS month and flooding it.

## The content engine: 1 pillar → 30 micro

Here's the system. Stop making one-off posts.

- **Pillar (1x/week):** sit down and record ONE longer thing. A 10-minute "I tracked 30 eSIM providers for a month, here's what I learned." Or a live screen-share comparing 6 countries. This is your raw material.
- **Micro (20-30 clips/week):** chop that pillar into shorts. Every price gap is a clip. Every tip is a clip. Every reaction is a clip. One pillar feeds a whole week of Reels, TikToks, Shorts, carousels, and tweets.
- **Reverse it too:** the best-performing micro clip becomes next week's pillar topic. The audience tells you what they want by what they share.

You're not "making content." You're running a media company that happens to sell eSIM comparison.

## The platform-by-platform attack (launch month)

**Instagram Reels + TikTok = your engine.** This is where the reach is. 1-2 a day, native to each. Backpackers live on TikTok, families and 25-40 live on Reels. Hit both.

**YouTube Shorts = the sleeper.** Same clips, but Shorts have a long tail and feed YouTube SEO. Someone Googling "eSIM Japan price" in 6 months can find your Short. Free compounding.

**Threads + X = real-time + screenshots.** Post the price gaps as text. "Same Japan eSIM: ₪35 vs ₪140." Screenshots of absurd price spreads get shared. Cheap to make, 3x a day.

**Facebook groups = the Israeli goldmine.** There are travel groups with hundreds of thousands of Israelis. DON'T spam a link. Show up, answer "what eSIM for Greece?" questions genuinely for two weeks, become known as the data person, THEN your tool gets pulled in naturally by you and by others. This is slow and it's the highest-trust channel you have.

**WhatsApp = the secret weapon in Israel.** Every video must be forwardable. The family group is the most powerful distribution network in this country and there's no algorithm to fight. Make a 12-second Hebrew clip whose only job is to be sent to "מי שטס." End every Israeli video with "שתפו."

**Reddit (English) = high-intent, low-tolerance.** r/travel, r/esim, r/solotravel. These people are BUYING. But they ban self-promo on sight. Give value for a week, disclose it's yours, never hard-sell. One good Reddit thread can outconvert 100 Reels because the intent is so high.

## The $1.80 strategy (this is the unglamorous gold)

Leave your two cents on the top 9 posts under 10 relevant hashtags, every day. That's $1.80 of value. #esim #travelhack #טיול #roaming #backpacking and so on.

Not "great post 🔥". REAL comments. Someone posts "off to Thailand!" → you reply "have the best time. one tip: don't buy data at the airport, it's 3x the price. compare eSIMs first." You're not pitching. You're being useful in public, on posts that ALREADY have the exact audience you want. Do this 30 minutes a day. It builds the brand one human at a time and it's free.

And: **reply to every single comment on your own posts, especially in the first hour.** Comments are rocket fuel for the algorithm and every reply is a relationship. When someone says "this saved me ₪300," reply, then screenshot it, then post the screenshot. The community manages the brand for you once you start.

## The launch sequence (first 30 days, concrete)

**Days 1-7: "We exist."**
- Drop the founder story video (you, real, why you built it). That's your manifesto.
- Personally DM/post it into 10 travel groups with a human note: "I built this, it's free, tell me if it's useful."
- 1 video/day. Reply to everything within the hour. Comment $1.80 daily.

**Days 8-14: Destination blitz.**
- One video per popular country, each with a live price reveal, each deep-linked to its page. Japan, Greece, Thailand, USA, Georgia, Dubai. People search by destination, meet them there.

**Days 15-21: Social proof loop.**
- Repost every "this saved me money." Run the "what's your worst roaming bill" UGC ask. Now you're posting OTHER people's content, which is the strongest signal there is.

**Days 22-30: Pour gas on the fire.**
- Look at your data. Whatever ONE video overperformed, put ₪50-100/day behind it and 3x the variations of that exact creative. Don't boost the losers to "be fair." Be ruthless. Feed the winner.

---

## When to spend money (and how little you need)

Organic first, ALWAYS. Money doesn't fix bad creative, it just buys you faster proof that it's bad. So you wait until the organic data tells you which video the market actually wants, and THEN you put a small budget behind that exact one to scale what's already working. You'll know you have a winner when a video does well with zero spend. That's the green light. Until then, your budget is your time and your phone.

---

## The KPIs I actually care about

Forget likes. Likes are vanity. I want:
- **Shares and saves.** A share means it was useful enough to risk their reputation forwarding it. That's the real vote.
- **Comments.** Conversation = the algorithm thinks it's worth showing more people.
- **Watch-through.** Did they stay past 3 seconds? Your hook is everything. If they bounce, the hook failed, not the product.
- **Clicks to the site + deals tapped.** The money metric. (Fix your tracking so you can actually see this - your own plan flags it.)

If a video gets 500 likes and 2 shares, it's a dud. If it gets 50 likes and 80 shares, you found gold. Make 10 more of that.

## The brand truth that makes this last

You have ONE unfair advantage and it's not the tech, it's that **you're the honest referee.** Every eSIM brand is screaming "buy ME." You're the only one saying "here's who's actually cheapest, including the guys who don't pay us the most." That's trust, and trust is the only thing that compounds in affiliate.

So protect it like your life depends on it. Never fake a price gap. Never bury the genuinely cheapest option because the commission is worse. The day you become "just another affiliate site" you're dead. The day people believe "MOCA always tells me the truth," you've won the category.
