# Viral Video Scripts - Short Form (Reels / TikTok / Shorts)

**Rules baked into every script below**
- Hook lands in the first 1.5 seconds. No intro, no logo, no "hey guys."
- Vertical 9:16. 15-35 seconds. On-screen text always (most watch muted).
- One idea per video. One CTA: *check before you fly, esim.mocaintel.com*.
- Caption deep-links to the destination shown (`?dest=...`) so they land on results.
- Native to the platform. Reshoot, don't crosspost with a TikTok watermark.

Format per script: **Hook → Shots → On-screen text → Voiceover → CTA → Why it works.**

## Script 1 - "The roaming bill" (the flagship)
**Format:** talking-to-camera + screen recording. **Length:** 25s. **Audience:** everyone.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text | VO / dialogue |

| 2-6s | Show the (mock/real) carrier bill | "Roaming. Every. Time." | "Roaming. I do this every single trip and I never learn." |
| 6-10s | Cut to screen recording of esim.mocaintel.com, type "יוון" | "There's a free site that fixes this" | "Then someone showed me this. It's free. You just pick where you're going." |
| 10-18s | Screen rec: results appear, finger taps the cheapest | "30+ providers, compared. Cheapest = ₪29" | "It compares like thirty eSIM companies and shows the cheapest one. Greece, a week of data: twenty-nine shekels." |
| 18-22s | Split screen: ₪612 vs ₪29 | "₪612 → ₪29" | "Six hundred and twelve... versus twenty-nine." |
| 22-25s | Back to face, point down | "esim.mocaintel.com (free)" | "Check it before your next flight. Link's right there." |

**Why it works:** loss → relief arc, a real number gap, the product does the reveal. The bill is the hook; the comparison is the payoff.

## Production notes (shoot all 7 in one session)
- **Batch it:** one outfit change, one afternoon, bank 15-20 cuts. Variation is free once you're set up.
- **Hooks are interchangeable:** test the same body with 3 different first lines. The hook is 80% of performance.
- **Screen recordings:** record the real `esim.mocaintel.com` flow at 60fps; the live "updated twice a day" pulse and the price sort are your best B-roll.
- **Captions:** burn them in (CapCut auto-captions), plus a written caption with the deep link and 3-5 hashtags (#esim #טיול #roaming #travelhack #חופש).
- **Thumbnail frame:** the price gap ("₪612 → ₪29") or the shocked face. Pick the frame deliberately.
- **CTA discipline:** one CTA, every time, same URL. Repetition is the point.
